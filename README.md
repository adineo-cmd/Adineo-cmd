I build things that make knowledge easier to find, check, and use: browsable
atlases, evidence-graded field guides, reproducible research labs, and
spec-first open-source software. Most of it is static-first, offline-capable,
and free of tracking.

Based in Kolkata, India. I work mainly in **Python**, **TypeScript** and
**JavaScript**, with Astro, FastAPI, SQLite and the occasional bit of Kotlin.
